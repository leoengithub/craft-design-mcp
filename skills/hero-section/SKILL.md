## Purpose
The hero is the first thing a user sees. Its only job is to communicate what the product does and drive one action. Every element competes for attention — fewer elements win.

## Required elements
- One headline (product's core value, specific not generic)
- One primary CTA button
- Optional: one subheadline (supporting, not repeating the headline)
- Optional: one visual (product screenshot, illustration, or none)

## Layout rules
- Single column, vertically centered or top-aligned
- CTA placed immediately after headline or subheadline — never below the fold
- Maximum visual weight on the headline — nothing competes with it
- If a visual is used, it supports the headline; it does not replace it

## Component patterns
- Flat CTA button — no gradient, no shadow, high contrast fill
- Headline: Display size (40–64px), medium or semibold weight
- Subheadline: Body size, muted color, shorter than the headline
- Background: solid color or very subtle texture — no hero images behind text

## Anti-patterns
- Two or more CTA buttons (primary + secondary) — pick one
- Carousels or animated hero slides
- Stock photography as background
- Purple or blue gradients
- Generic taglines: "The future of X", "Revolutionizing Y", "The all-in-one Z"
- Invented social proof: fake logos, made-up user counts, placeholder testimonials
- Illustration of hand-drawn humans
- Subheadline longer than the headline
- CTA label: "Get started" — too generic; use the specific action

## Self-critique checklist
- [ ] Philosophy: Does the headline communicate what the product actually does (not what it aspires to)?
- [ ] Hierarchy: Is the CTA visually dominant after the headline?
- [ ] Specificity: Is the headline specific to this product, or could it apply to any SaaS?
- [ ] Restraint: Is there exactly one CTA? At most one visual element?
- [ ] Completeness: Are headline and CTA present? Is the background non-competing?
- [ ] P0: Exactly one CTA button — not two, not zero
- [ ] P0: No stock photography, gradient backgrounds, or placeholder content

## Example prompts
- "Hero for a B2B project management tool — primary action is starting a free trial"
- "Hero for a developer API product — primary action is viewing the docs"
- "Pre-launch hero for a mobile app — primary action is joining the waitlist"
