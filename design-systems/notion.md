## Typography

| Role        | Size  | Weight | Usage                        |
|-------------|-------|--------|------------------------------|

| Subheadline | 20px  | 600    | Subsection titles            |

| Caption     | 12px  | 400    | Fine print, metadata         |

Typeface: Inter (fallback: system-ui)

## Spacing

Base unit: 4px
Scale: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96

## Layout

- Max content width: 900px (content-width pages), 1200px (marketing)
- Page padding: 24px (mobile) / 96px (desktop)
- Section vertical padding: 80px (desktop) / 48px (mobile)
- Column grid: generous margins, single-column content preferred

## Components

- Buttons: minimal border, 6px radius, Label size
- Cards: subtle border or background fill, 8px radius
- Inputs: light border, 6px radius, 36px height
- Callouts: background-fill boxes with icon

## Motion

- Default duration: 200ms
- Easing: ease-in-out
- Gentle transitions — no abrupt changes

## Voice

- Warm and approachable — not cold or corporate
- Content-first — the design serves the content
- Familiar without being casual

## Brand

- Warmth through color temperature (off-whites, not pure white)
- Typography does the heavy lifting — minimal decoration
- Generous whitespace — content breathes

## Anti-patterns
- High-contrast aggressive colors
- Dense information layouts
- Hard-edged geometric decoration
- Cold pure-white backgrounds
- Small tight spacing
